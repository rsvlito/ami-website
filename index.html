OUR STORY

Rooted in Roseburg,
Inspired by Asia.

Since opening our doors in 2006, Ami Sushi has been proud to serve Roseburg with fresh ingredients, big flavors, and genuine hospitality.

What began as a local sushi restaurant has grown into an Asian-Inspired Kitchen—bringing together Japanese favorites, Korean flavors, and new creations inspired by our community.

We are grateful for every guest who has been part of our journey.
